Upload your demand history

Drop an Excel file in the sidebar and MiniForecast will train 8 forecasting models, score them on a holdout window, pick the best one, and project your horizon.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the file is parsed and forecast entirely inside your browser. No install, no account, no server.

Expected format

A date column plus one or more numeric columns. Add extra product columns to use batch mode.

DateProduct_AProduct_BProduct_C
2020-01-0110255081940
2020-02-0111025282302
…………
Needs at least 12 data points per product; 36+ monthly points gives the seasonal models enough to work with.
